# Document-Type Checklists

Each document type has a checklist that WISE shows when the document is registered
(Phase 1: flags gaps only) or created (Phase 3 and later: system gates enforce it).
Notation: **[A]** = system checks it automatically once the data is structured; **[M]** = a person confirms it.
All checklists are versioned. Items marked *(verify)* must be confirmed by TESDA Legal/BAC Sec/Accounting before activation.

Common items for **every** document (Phase 1 registration):
- [M] Document type correctly identified; reference number and date legible
- [M] All pages scanned, readable, in correct order
- [M] Signatures present for the current stage; no erasures without initials
- [A] Tracking No. issued; originating office and PAP tagged
- [M] Sensitive personal data flagged (passport, minors, medical) for restricted access

---

## A. Planning and Performance

### A1. Work and Financial Plan (WFP)
- [A] FY, fund source, PAP (PREXC/UACS) per line
- [A] Total per PAP = allotment (GAA/release document)
- [M] Physical targets per PAP match the BED Physical Plan / OPCR
- [M] GAD-attributed activities tagged
- Signatories: Prepared (Secretariat) → Reviewed (Planning, Budget) → Approved (Head of Office)

### A2. OPCR / A3. DPCR
- [A] Rating period (semester) stated
- [A] Each success indicator linked to a PAP / higher-level indicator
- [M] Targets have quality/efficiency/timeliness measures per SPMS
- [M] Budget and responsible unit indicated per MFO/PAP
- Signatories: Head of Office (commitment) → PMT/Planning (review) → Head of Agency or delegate (approval)

### A4. IPCR
- [A] Each success indicator linked to a DPCR/OPCR indicator
- [M] Targets are measurable; MOV defined per indicator
- Signatories: Ratee → Immediate Supervisor → Head of Office

### A5. Monthly Accomplishment Report (Phase 1)
- [A] Linked to IPCR indicator(s); reporting month stated
- [A] At least one MOV per reported output
- [M] Output quantity/quality consistent with MOV
- Signatories: Staff → Immediate Supervisor (confirmation)

### A6. Means of Verification (MOV)
- [M] Dated, identifies activity/output, legible
- [M] Attendance sheets, photos, reports, or a link to a tracked document
- [M] No personal data beyond what is needed

## B. Procurement Planning

### B1. Market Scoping Report
- [M] At least the number of sources the IRR requires *(verify)*; sources identified (PhilGEPS, suppliers, PS-DBM price list, prior contracts)
- [M] Date of scoping within the validity period; prices comparable (same specs)
- [M] Sustainable/green and local-content options considered where applicable

### B2. Project Procurement Management Plan (PPMP)
- [A] Indicative / Final / Supplemental type stated
- [A] Each line: item/project, UACS object code, quantity, estimated cost, mode, schedule, PAP, linked WFP line
- [A] Total ≤ WFP amount for the same PAP/object code
- [M] Market scoping attached for the basis of estimated cost
- Signatories: End-user → Head of Office; reviewed by Budget

### B3. Annual Procurement Plan (APP)
- [A] Consolidates all approved PPMPs; totals match
- [A] Mode, schedule, ABC, and fund source per project
- [M] Approved by the HoPE; posted on PhilGEPS and the agency website within the required period *(verify)*
- Signatories: BAC Secretariat (prepared) → BAC (recommended) → HoPE (approved)

### B4. APP-CSE
- [M] Uses the current PS-DBM template; quantities per quarter
- [M] Submitted through the PS-DBM Virtual Store by the DBM deadline
- Signatories: Supply/Property Officer → Budget → Accountant → HoPE (per PS-DBM form)

## C. Request Preparation and Review

### C1. Purchase Request (PR)
- [A] PR no., date, office, PAP/UACS, **APP line reference**
- [A] Items, unit, quantity, unit cost, total; total ≤ remaining APP line balance
- [M] Purpose stated and consistent with the PPMP/APP entry
- Attachments: TOR or Technical Specifications; ABC computation; Market Scoping Report
- Signatories: Requested by (End-user) → Funds available/earmarked (Budget) → Approved by (per Delegation of Authority)

### C2. Terms of Reference / Technical Specifications
- [M] Performance/functional specs; **no brand names** unless allowed by the IRR
- [M] Scope, deliverables, delivery schedule/place, warranty, qualifications of supplier/consultant
- [M] Evaluation criteria (e.g., lowest calculated responsive bid, MEARB, quality-cost) stated where applicable
- [M] Approved by the end-user head / TWG

### C3. ABC Computation
- [A] Based on market scoping; within the APP/PPMP estimated cost
- [M] Includes taxes, delivery, and incidental costs

---

## D. Procurement Processing

### D1. Request for Quotation / Invitation to Bid
- [A] Linked to an approved PR; ABC stated
- [M] Mode of procurement justified per RA 12009 IRR (attach BAC resolution where required)
- [M] PhilGEPS posting reference and posting period met *(verify)*

### D2. Abstract of Quotations / Bids
- [A] Lists all bidders/suppliers who responded; amounts computed correctly
- [M] Compliance with specs checked per bidder
- Signatories: BAC members / TWG

### D3. Post-Qualification Report (where applicable)
- [M] Legal, technical, and financial documents verified; site/reference checks where required

### D4. BAC Resolution (recommending award / declaring mode / failure)
- [A] Linked to the abstract/post-qual report
- [M] Quorum met; basis of recommendation stated
- Signatories: BAC Chair, members → HoPE approval

### D5. Notice of Award (NOA)
- [A] Contract amount ≤ ABC; linked to the approved BAC resolution
- [M] Posted on PhilGEPS as required; conformed by the winning bidder
- [M] Performance security requirement stated

---

## E. Budget Obligation

### E1. Obligation Request and Status (ORS) / BURS
- [A] Payee, particulars, UACS object code, PAP, amount
- [A] Amount ≤ unobligated allotment; linked to NOA/PO/contract or legal basis document (non-procurement)
- [M] Box A: certified by the requesting head (charges necessary, lawful, under their direct supervision; supporting documents valid)
- [M] Box B: certified by the Budget Officer (allotment available and obligated)
- Note: BURS instead of ORS where the GAM prescribes it for the fund source *(verify)*

### E2. Certificate of Availability of Funds (CAF)
- [M] Signed by the Chief Accountant before contract/PO signing (PD 1445 Sec. 86–87)

---

## F. Contract and Implementation

### F1. Purchase Order / Contract
- [A] **ORS and CAF certified before signing date** (gate G5)
- [A] Amount = NOA amount; delivery terms consistent with the TOR
- [M] Performance security posted (form and amount per IRR); warranty terms
- [M] Copy sent to the COA Audit Team within the required period (COA Circ. 2009-001)
- Signatories: HoPE/authorized official; supplier conformity

### F2. Notice to Proceed (NTP)
- [A] Issued after contract signing; date of receipt by the supplier recorded

### F3. Delivery Receipt / Invoice
- [A] References the PO/contract; quantities ≤ ordered
- [M] **Invoice** (BIR-registered; RA 11976) instead of official receipt as primary evidence

### F4. Inspection and Acceptance Report (IAR)
- [A] References the PO, invoice, and delivery receipt; delivery date (for liquidated damages)
- [M] Inspection: Inspection Committee verifies quantity and specifications
- [M] Acceptance: Supply/Property Officer accepts complete/partial delivery
- [A] Late delivery → liquidated damages computed per IRR/contract

### F5. PAR / ICS / RIS
- [A] PAR if unit cost ≥ ₱50,000 (PPE); ICS if below (semi-expendable) (COA Circ. 2022-004)
- [M] Accountable officer signs; property number assigned
- [M] RIS for supplies issued from stock

---

## G. Accounting and Payment

### G1. Disbursement Voucher (DV) — Goods/Services (procured)
- [A] Linked to ORS/BURS; amount ≤ obligation balance; payee matches contract
- Attachments (COA Circ. 2012-001, as amended — verify per mode):
  - [M] Approved contract/PO, NOA, NTP (as applicable), BAC resolution, abstract
  - [M] ORS/BURS
  - [M] IAR (inspected and accepted); delivery receipt; invoice
  - [M] PhilGEPS posting evidence (as applicable)
  - [M] Performance security/warranty security (as applicable)
  - [M] Liquidated damages computation (if late)
- [M] Withholding taxes computed; retention withheld where required
- Signatories: Box A (Head of requesting unit) → Box C (Chief Accountant) → Box D (Approving Official) → Box E (payee receipt, for check payment)

### G2. LDDAP-ADA / Check
- [A] Matches approved DVs; total within available NCA
- [M] Signed by authorized signatories; bank-validated

---

## H. Non-Procurement (WSP Operations)

### H1. Travel Order / Authority
- [A] Traveler, destination, dates, purpose, fund source, estimated cost
- [M] Foreign travel: travel authority from the proper approving level *(verify current OP/DBM rules)*
- [M] Rates per EO 77 s. 2019

### H2. Cash Advance Request
- [A] Requesting officer has **no overdue unliquidated CA** (gate G9)
- [M] Officer is designated/bonded as Special Disbursing Officer where required
- [M] Approved travel order/activity plan and estimated budget attached

### H3. Liquidation Report
- [A] Filed within the required period (local vs. foreign travel differ — COA Circ. 97-002, *verify*)
- [M] Receipts/invoices; Certificate of Travel Completed; itinerary; boarding passes/tickets; RER where allowed
- [M] Refund of excess cash advance (official receipt from the Cashier) if any

### H4. Honoraria Payroll
- [M] Office order/designation of experts, judges, resource persons
- [M] Legal basis and approved rates *(verify current DBM circulars)*
- [M] Proof of attendance/output; tax withheld

### H5. Competitor Allowance / Incentive Payroll
- [M] Legal/policy basis (GAA provision, TESDA Board resolution or approved policy) *(confirm with Legal)*
- [M] Approved list of recipients; IDs; bank details; parental consent for minors

### H6. WSI / WorldSkills Asia Membership or Participation Fee
- [M] Membership basis/agreement; WSI invoice
- [M] Foreign exchange computation per Accounting rules; approval

---

## I. Monitoring, Audit and Closure

### I1. Procurement Monitoring Report (PMR)
- [A] Covers all procurement activities in the period; GPPB format
- [M] Submitted and posted within the GPPB deadline

### I2. Budget/Financial Accountability Reports (BAR/FAR)
- [A] Generated from obligation and disbursement registers
- [M] Reconciled between Budget and Accounting before submission

### I3. Project Completion / Closure Report
- [A] All DVs paid; warranty period ended; retention and securities released
- [M] No open AOM/NS/ND for the transaction
- [M] Records transmitted and indexed per the Records Disposition Schedule
